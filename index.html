Step 3 of 4 — What We Do

Plain work. Plain list.

Four groups of work. No upsells hiding in the fine print.

Site Work

  • Redesign on your existing WordPress + Elementor setup. No platform migration. You keep what works.
  • New page builds — including the consulting page this proposal is really about.
  • Mobile polish, so it behaves on the phones your clients actually use.
  • A speed pass. Faster loads, same content.
  • Quiet hardening: small things that make your site harder to poke at.

Launch Work

  • Consulting-offer page copy, written alongside you. You know UX writing; we just help you aim it.
  • A simple intake or booking form, wired to your email.
  • Newsletter hookup, if you want one. No pressure if you don't.
  • Analytics baseline, so you can see what's happening from day one.

Ongoing Care

  • A monthly care plan: updates, backups, small fixes.
  • Content edits when your work or offer changes.
  • New pages as you grow. Adding a page shouldn't require a project plan.
  • Emergency help when something breaks. Because someday it will.

Trade

  • Your design and brand assets in exchange for tech hours. Figma and Illustrator work counts, at your level and rates.
  • Open to a trade — tell us what you need, and we'll shape the swap together.
